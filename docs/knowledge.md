# Notes

This file is shown in the app's third sidebar tab, on wide screens, next to
whatever article you are reading. Replace it with your own running notes: what
you now believe about your field, what the open questions are, which numbers you
keep having to look up.

The point of having it there is comparison. When a new paper claims something,
you want your current position visible in the same glance, not in another
window — that is what makes you notice the moment a paper contradicts you.

Rename it with `knowledge_file:` in config.yaml. It has to live in `docs/`, since
that is what the browser can reach.
